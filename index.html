About AR Car Rentals

AR Car Rentals is a brand new car rental service proudly serving the states of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. We provide premium vehicles along with professional, experienced drivers who are well-versed with every route across both states. Whether you're traveling for business, family trips, weddings, pilgrimages, or any other purpose, we have the right car and driver for you.

Our fleet includes Sedans, SUVs, Luxury cars, Hatchbacks, MUVs, Minivans and more. All our vehicles are well-maintained, insured, and equipped for comfortable long-distance travel. Our drivers speak Telugu, Hindi, English, and Urdu to ensure smooth communication.
